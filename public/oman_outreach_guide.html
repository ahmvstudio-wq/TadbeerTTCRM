Outreach rules depend entirely on the target channel.

💼 LinkedIn

📨 Opener Rule: Specific Observation > Praise

Standard flattery ("your profile looks beautiful") is ignored. Instead, open with a **low-friction observation** of a specific element that shows you did your research:

❌ Generic Praise (Sells Nothing)
"The packaging details look very polished on your feed."
"The room layout looks clean on your maps profile."
✅ Specific Observation
"The typography detailing on your [product/bean] packaging matches the premium positioning well."
"I noticed the [treatment name] transformation cases you posted yesterday show exceptionally natural skin textures."

🤝 Handling Responses (The Oman Scriptbook)
How to handle: "How much is this?"
"It depends on what makes sense for your business. Some of our clients work with us on a monthly marketing and systems retainer. Others prefer a project-based approach where we build something specific and hand it over. I'd rather understand your situation properly before throwing out a number — can we sit down and figure out what would actually move the needle for you?"

Rule: Never quote prices in chat. The pricing conversation happens face-to-face.

How to handle: "Not interested."
"Completely understood. If anything changes or if you'd like a second opinion on anything in the future, I'm always around. Wishing you and the team continued success."

Rule: Respect it immediately. Do not push. Revisit in 60-90 days with fresh value.

How to handle: "We already work with an agency."
"That's great — having someone handling that is important. I'm not suggesting replacing anyone. I'm more curious about whether you're seeing the results you're hoping for in terms of actual booked appointments coming through. If things are working well, that's brilliant. If there are gaps, happy to share some thoughts."

Rule: Don't badmouth competitors. Shift focus to conversion/business results.
