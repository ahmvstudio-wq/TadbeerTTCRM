## 02 | FORENSIC DIAGNOSIS — OPERATIONAL LEAKS

Mwasalat operates 400+ buses across Muscat governorate routes, inter-city services to Sohar/Nizwa/Salalah, school contracts, and Sultan Qaboos University services carrying 1.1M+ passengers annually. Managing route efficiency, fuel consumption, vehicle maintenance, and passenger demand across 40+ routes with government subsidy pressure requires data-driven optimization. Current static scheduling leaves 30-40% capacity underutilized while other routes overcrowded.

### CRITICAL OPERATIONAL LEAKS

#### LEAK 01

**LEAK:** Fuel Waste from Suboptimal Routing & Idling

**RISK:** Fuel represents 69% of fleet operating costs. Inefficient routes, traffic congestion, and excessive idling waste 15-20% of fuel budget (RO 4.2M annually).

**GAP:** Static routes cannot adapt to real-time traffic conditions, road closures, weather, or events. No AI system optimizing routes dynamically for minimum fuel consumption.

---

#### LEAK 02

**LEAK:** Unbalanced Capacity Utilization & Service Quality

**RISK:** Peak hour routes overcrowded (150%+ capacity) while off-peak and weekend services run 30-40% empty. Passenger complaints about overcrowding and unreliable schedules.

**GAP:** Manual route planning cannot predict demand patterns by time, day, season, weather, and events to optimize bus frequency and capacity deployment.

---

#### LEAK 03

**LEAK:** Reactive Maintenance & Unplanned Vehicle Downtime

**RISK:** 15-20% of fleet unavailable due to unscheduled maintenance. Breakdowns disrupt service reliability. Maintenance costs RO 2.6M annually, 30% higher than predictive approach.

**GAP:** Calendar-based preventive maintenance over-services some vehicles and under-services others. No IoT sensors and AI predicting component failures before breakdowns occur.

## 03 | SOLUTION — IMPLEMENTATION PHASES

WOS deploys Public Transit AI Platform combining dynamic route optimization, predictive maintenance, and passenger demand forecasting—specifically calibrated for mixed-use fleets serving urban and inter-city routes in GCC conditions.

### Phase 01
**AI Route Optimization & Dynamic Scheduling**

Deploy real-time route optimization AI integrating GPS fleet tracking, traffic conditions, weather forecasts, and special events to dynamically adjust routes for minimum travel time and fuel consumption. Machine learning models predict traffic patterns by time of day, day of week, and seasonal factors with 95% accuracy. System recommends route adjustments when: traffic accidents block primary routes, construction zones create detours, special events generate demand surges, weather conditions affect driving speeds. Integration with passenger mobile app provides real-time arrival predictions and alternative route suggestions during disruptions. Genetic algorithms optimize bus frequency and timing across 40+ routes balancing passenger wait times, vehicle utilization, and operating costs. Expected outcome: 18% reduction in fuel costs (RO 3.2M savings), 35% improvement in on-time performance, 25% reduction in passenger complaints, 20% decrease in average journey times.

---

### Phase 02
**Passenger Demand Forecasting & Capacity Planning**

Implement AI system analyzing 10+ years of ridership data, mobile ticketing patterns, seasonal trends, weather conditions, construction projects, university academic calendars, Ramadan schedules, and government holiday impacts to forecast passenger demand at route and time-slot granularity. Predictive models identify: peak demand periods requiring additional buses, low-utilization time slots for frequency reduction, route segments with chronic overcrowding, new route opportunities in underserved areas. Dynamic capacity allocation recommends optimal bus size (28-seat, 45-seat, articulated) and frequency per route. Integration with Oman Tender Board construction data predicts detours and demand shifts. School and SQU contract optimization ensures buses deployed where needed most. Expected outcome: 28% improvement in capacity utilization, 40% reduction in empty-seat kilometers, RO 1.8M subsidy efficiency gain, 35% increase in passenger satisfaction scores.

---

### Phase 03
**Predictive Maintenance & Fleet Health Monitoring**

Deploy IoT sensor network on 400+ buses monitoring engine performance, transmission health, brake wear, tire pressure, battery voltage, coolant temperature, and oil quality. AI analyzes sensor data streams, maintenance history, and driving patterns to predict component failures 14-21 days in advance. Automated work orders schedule maintenance during off-peak hours minimizing service disruptions. Parts inventory optimization ensures critical components in stock before needed. Driver behavior monitoring identifies harsh braking, rapid acceleration, excessive idling reducing component lifespan. Mobile app for mechanics shows prioritized maintenance tasks with diagnostic codes and recommended actions. Integration with bus availability forecasting enables route planners to account for scheduled maintenance windows. Expected outcome: 60% reduction in unplanned downtime, 30% decrease in maintenance costs (RO 780K savings), 15% extension of vehicle lifespan, 95% fleet availability rate vs current 80-85%.
