## 02 | FORENSIC DIAGNOSIS — OPERATIONAL LEAKS

Arak Medical Clinics operates multi-specialty facilities (dermatology, aesthetics, laser, dentistry) across Al Azaiba, Al Khoud, and Bousher with extended hours (7 AM - 10 PM). Managing appointment scheduling, patient reminders, follow-up care, and front-desk operations for 3 locations creates administrative burden consuming 40% of staff time and causing 25-30% no-show rates worth RO 450K annually in lost revenue.

### CRITICAL OPERATIONAL LEAKS

#### LEAK 01

**LEAK:** High Appointment No-Show Rate & Last-Minute Cancellations

**RISK:** 25-30% no-show rate across dermatology and dental services wastes physician capacity worth RO 450K annually. Cannot backfill slots with short notice.

**GAP:** Manual SMS reminders sent day-before only. No AI system sending multi-channel reminders (WhatsApp, SMS, calls) at optimal times based on patient behavior patterns.

---

#### LEAK 02

**LEAK:** Phone-Dependent Booking Creating Staff Bottlenecks

**RISK:** Patients must call during business hours to schedule. 40% of calls go to voicemail during peak times. Staff spend 35% of time on booking/rescheduling instead of patient care.

**GAP:** No 24/7 AI chatbot enabling self-service appointment booking, rescheduling, and cancellation via website and WhatsApp without staff involvement.

---

#### LEAK 03

**LEAK:** Inconsistent Post-Treatment Follow-Up & Patient Retention

**RISK:** Manual follow-up for dermatology treatments, dental procedures, and aesthetic services reaches only 30% of patients. Lost repeat revenue worth RO 285K annually.

**GAP:** No automated system sending post-visit care instructions, medication reminders, feedback surveys, and rebooking prompts based on treatment protocols.

## 03 | SOLUTION — IMPLEMENTATION PHASES

WOS deploys Healthcare AI Engagement Platform combining automated appointment management, multi-channel patient communication, and intelligent follow-up orchestration—HIPAA-compliant and optimized for multi-specialty ambulatory clinics.

### Phase 01
**AI Appointment Scheduling & Automated Reminders**

Deploy conversational AI on Arak website, WhatsApp Business, and Facebook Messenger enabling 24/7 self-service appointment booking. Patients select specialty (dermatology, dental, aesthetics, laser), preferred doctor, location, and date/time. AI checks real-time physician availability across 3 clinics and books appointments directly into clinic management system. Automated confirmation via patient preferred channel (WhatsApp, SMS, email). Intelligent reminder system sends: 1-week advance reminder, 24-hour reminder, 2-hour final reminder with one-click rescheduling option. AI predicts no-show probability per appointment based on patient history and triggers additional outreach for high-risk bookings. Expected outcome: 80% of appointments self-booked without staff intervention, 50% reduction in no-show rate, RO 380K revenue recovery, 70% decrease in front-desk phone volume.

---

### Phase 02
**Patient Communication AI & Multi-Channel Engagement**

Implement AI system managing all patient communications: appointment confirmations, pre-visit instructions (fasting requirements, medication holds), post-visit care protocols, medication schedules, and follow-up reminders. Integration with dermatology, dental, and aesthetics treatment protocols ensures specialty-specific guidance. AI chatbot handles common FAQs (services offered, insurance accepted, parking, hours, pricing) reducing staff inquiry volume by 75%. Sentiment analysis monitors patient messages and escalates concerns or complaints to clinic manager immediately. Patient portal integration allows secure document sharing (lab results, prescriptions, treatment photos). Automated feedback surveys capture patient satisfaction and generate Google/Facebook reviews. Expected outcome: 90% of routine inquiries handled by AI, 40% improvement in patient satisfaction scores, 85% reduction in administrative phone time.

---

### Phase 03
**Predictive Follow-Up & Patient Retention Intelligence**

Deploy AI nurture engine that automatically schedules follow-up communications based on treatment protocols and patient behavior. Dermatology patients receive skincare routine reminders and 3-month follow-up prompts. Dental patients get post-procedure care instructions and 6-month cleaning reminders. Aesthetics clients receive treatment result check-ins and next session recommendations. Churn prediction identifies patients overdue for routine care and triggers win-back campaigns with appointment incentives. Lifetime value scoring helps prioritize high-value patient retention efforts. Integration with inventory system enables automated rebooking when new laser equipment or aesthetic services launch. Expected outcome: 60% improvement in follow-up consistency, 45% increase in repeat visit rate, RO 285K annual retention revenue, 25% growth in treatment package sales.
