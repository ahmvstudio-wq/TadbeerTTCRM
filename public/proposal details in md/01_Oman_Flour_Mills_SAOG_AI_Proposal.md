## 02 | FORENSIC DIAGNOSIS — OPERATIONAL LEAKS

Oman Flour Mills operates at 800 MT/day flour capacity and 1500 MT/day animal feed capacity across multiple production lines. With 1,100+ employees, RO 68M+ turnover, and government shareholding, maintaining consistent quality while scaling production presents critical challenges. Current reactive maintenance and manual quality checks create margin leakage estimated at 8-12% of production value annually.

### CRITICAL OPERATIONAL LEAKS

#### LEAK 01

**LEAK:** Quality Defects Detected at End-of-Line

**RISK:** Entire batch rejections result in 2-5% product scrap worth RO 1.2M+ annually. Late detection wastes raw materials, production time, and creates delivery delays.

**GAP:** No predictive quality system analyzing production parameters in real-time. Manual inspection cannot forecast degradation 30-60 minutes before completion.

---

#### LEAK 02

**LEAK:** Unplanned Equipment Downtime

**RISK:** Milling machinery failures cause 15-25% unplanned downtime worth RO 2.8M in lost production. Disrupts Dahabi and Barakat delivery schedules.

**GAP:** Reactive maintenance strategy lacks predictive analytics on equipment health, bearing wear patterns, motor degradation, and belt tension anomalies.

---

#### LEAK 03

**LEAK:** Inconsistent Production Parameters

**RISK:** Manual parameter adjustments cause batch-to-batch variations affecting brand consistency. Energy waste from non-optimized settings adds RO 450K annually.

**GAP:** No AI optimization adjusting temperature, pressure, mixing ratios based on real-time ingredient quality variations from different wheat suppliers.

## 03 | SOLUTION — IMPLEMENTATION PHASES

WOS deploys an integrated AI Manufacturing Intelligence Platform combining computer vision quality prediction, IoT-powered predictive maintenance, and self-optimizing production control—specifically calibrated for food processing environments with GMP compliance.

### Phase 01
**AI Quality Prediction & Real-Time Intervention**

Deploy computer vision cameras and IoT sensors monitoring flour particle size distribution, moisture content, temperature profiles, mixing consistency, and packaging integrity. Machine learning models trained on 18+ months of production data predict quality defects up to 60 minutes before batch completion with 95% accuracy. When parameters drift outside optimal ranges, system auto-alerts operators via mobile app with specific adjustment recommendations. Integration with SCADA enables automated micro-adjustments to milling speed and temperature. Expected outcome: 60% reduction in batch rejections, 81% improvement in defect detection, RO 1.2M annual savings.

---

### Phase 02
**Predictive Maintenance Intelligence System**

Install wireless IoT sensor network on 45+ critical assets: milling equipment, motors, conveyors, elevators, packaging lines, and silos. AI analyzes vibration signatures, temperature anomalies, current draw patterns, acoustic emissions, and bearing health to predict failures 7-14 days in advance. Automated work order generation in CMMS with specific component identification and failure probability. Parts inventory integration triggers automatic reordering of critical spare parts. Mobile dashboard for maintenance teams with prioritized intervention schedules. Expected outcome: 70% reduction in unplanned downtime, 30% decrease in maintenance costs, RO 2.8M annual savings.

---

### Phase 03
**Self-Optimizing Production Control AI**

Implement reinforcement learning system that continuously adjusts production parameters for maximum yield, minimum waste, and optimal energy consumption. AI evaluates incoming grain quality variations (protein content, moisture, foreign matter) from different suppliers and auto-calibrates milling settings. Genetic algorithms optimize production scheduling across flour and feed lines to minimize changeover waste and maximize facility utilization. Real-time executive dashboards display OEE, quality trends, energy consumption per ton, and predictive alerts. Expected outcome: 12% improvement in overall equipment effectiveness, 8% energy savings, 38% increase in production volume capacity utilization.
