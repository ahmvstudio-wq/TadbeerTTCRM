## 02 | FORENSIC DIAGNOSIS — OPERATIONAL LEAKS

National Finance, established 1987, is Oman leading non-banking finance company with 23+ branches serving retail and SME customers. Processing 15,000+ loan applications monthly with manual underwriting creates 2-5 day decision cycles, 40% auto-decision rates, and excludes creditworthy applicants with thin files. Current processes leave RO 8-12M in addressable lending capacity untapped while consuming excessive underwriting labor.

### CRITICAL OPERATIONAL LEAKS

#### LEAK 01

**LEAK:** Slow Loan Decisioning & Customer Abandonment

**RISK:** Manual underwriting takes 2-5 days. 35% of applicants abandon process before decision. Lost loan origination worth RO 6.5M annually.

**GAP:** Only 40% of applications auto-decisioned. No AI credit scoring analyzing alternative data (utility payments, mobile usage, employment verification) to assess thin-file borrowers.

---

#### LEAK 02

**LEAK:** Conservative Approval Rates & Missed Good Borrowers

**RISK:** Traditional credit scoring rejects 60% of applicants including viable thin-file borrowers (young professionals, new-to-Oman expats). RO 8.2M addressable lending opportunity lost.

**GAP:** FICO-only scoring misses creditworthy applicants without traditional credit history. No ML models trained on National Finance 35+ years of actual repayment performance data.

---

#### LEAK 03

**LEAK:** Inefficient Underwriter Utilization & Operational Costs

**RISK:** Underwriters spend 70% of time on routine low-risk applications that could be auto-decisioned. Labor costs RO 2.8M annually for work AI could handle.

**GAP:** Manual processing of straightforward approvals prevents underwriters from focusing on complex SME lending and relationship management where human judgment adds value.

## 03 | SOLUTION — IMPLEMENTATION PHASES

WOS implements AI Lending Intelligence Platform combining alternative data credit scoring, automated decisioning, and explainable risk assessment—compliant with Central Bank of Oman regulations and optimized for personal and SME lending in GCC markets.

### Phase 01
**AI Credit Scoring & Alternative Data Integration**

Deploy ensemble machine learning models (XGBoost, neural networks, gradient boosting) trained on National Finance 35+ years of actual loan performance data covering 200,000+ borrowers. Models analyze traditional credit bureau data plus alternative signals: salary transfer consistency, utility payment history, mobile phone billing patterns, social media professional profiles, employment verification APIs, rental payment records, and business transaction patterns for SMEs. Explainable AI (SHAP values) provides transparent reasoning for every decision meeting Central Bank audit requirements. Risk segmentation produces probability of default scores with confidence intervals. Integration with existing loan origination system enables real-time API decisioning in under 60 seconds. Expected outcome: 95% decision accuracy, 30% higher approval rates without increasing risk, RO 8.2M incremental lending capacity, 98% of Omani adults accessible for credit evaluation.

---

### Phase 02
**Automated Decisioning Engine & Smart Routing**

Implement AI decisioning platform that automatically approves 70-80% of applications meeting risk thresholds without human intervention. Loan amounts up to RO 15,000 for retail and RO 75,000 for SMEs can be fully automated. Borderline cases intelligently routed to underwriters with AI-generated risk summaries highlighting key approval/decline factors. Dynamic pricing engine recommends optimal interest rates balancing competitive positioning with risk-adjusted returns. Fraud detection algorithms flag synthetic identities, duplicate applications, and suspicious patterns. Real-time compliance checks ensure all automated decisions meet regulatory requirements. Mobile app integration provides instant pre-approval decisions for marketing campaigns. Expected outcome: 77% auto-decision rate, 90% reduction in decision time (5 days to 4 hours), 35% abandonment rate reduction, RO 6.5M recovered lost applications.

---

### Phase 03
**Portfolio Risk Monitoring & Early Warning System**

Deploy continuous portfolio monitoring AI analyzing repayment patterns, employment status changes, and economic indicators to predict default risk 60-90 days in advance. Early warning alerts enable proactive customer outreach and workout arrangements before delinquency. Automated payment reminder sequences via SMS and WhatsApp reduce involuntary defaults. Behavioral segmentation identifies customers likely to refinance, prepay, or need credit limit increases. Collection prioritization algorithm ranks delinquent accounts by recovery probability and optimal contact strategy. Integration with SMS gateway enables automated payment reminders and self-service payment plan setup. Expected outcome: 20% reduction in default rates, 25% improvement in collection efficiency, RO 2.1M reduction in write-offs, 50% decrease in underwriting labor costs (RO 1.4M savings).
